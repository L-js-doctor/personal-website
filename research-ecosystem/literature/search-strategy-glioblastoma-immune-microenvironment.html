Search Strategy

Glioblastoma Immune Microenvironment

A reusable PubMed search strategy for finding reviews and mechanistic papers related to glioblastoma, immunosuppression, oncolytic virus therapy, and tumor microenvironment remodeling.

Core Question

How do therapeutic strategies reprogram the immunosuppressive glioblastoma microenvironment, and what mechanisms should be tracked across reviews, trials, and experimental papers?

Primary PubMed Query

glioblastoma[Title/Abstract] AND immunosuppressive microenvironment[Title/Abstract] AND (oncolytic virus[Title/Abstract] OR immunotherapy[Title/Abstract]) AND review[Publication Type] AND (2020:2026[Date - Publication])

Expansion Terms

  • glioma vaccine
  • oncolytic herpes simplex virus
  • tumor-associated macrophage
  • microglia
  • immune checkpoint resistance
  • T cell exhaustion

Screening Rules

  1. Prioritize systematic reviews and high-quality mechanistic reviews for map building.
  2. Keep clinical trials separate from mechanism reviews.
  3. Save papers that clearly connect therapy to immune-cell or cytokine changes.
  4. Reject papers that mention immunotherapy only as background without mechanism detail.
